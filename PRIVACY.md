## 7. Your Rights Under the GDPR (Art. 15–22 GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

1. **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):**  
   You have the right to request confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed, and to receive information about that processing along with a copy of the data.

2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):**  
   You have the right to obtain without undue delay the rectification of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete personal data.

3. **Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17 GDPR):**  
   You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where one of the grounds specified in Art. 17(1) GDPR applies (e.g., the data is no longer necessary for the original purposes).

4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):**  
   You have the right to obtain restriction of processing under certain conditions (e.g., while the accuracy of the data is being contested).

5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR):**  
   You have the right to receive the personal data you provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, or have it transmitted directly to another controller where technically feasible.

6. **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):**  
   Where data processing is based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data on grounds relating to your particular situation.

7. **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR):**  
   Where processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR or Section 25(1) TDDDG), you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time with future effect.

8. **Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority (Art. 77 GDPR):**  
   If you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes data protection regulations, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.
